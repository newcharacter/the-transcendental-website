The Transcendental Motherfucking Website

We have reached the peak. We are done screaming. The anger of the previous iterations was a necessary step in our evolution, but it was just that: a step. We are no longer defined by what we hate (bloat, tracking, divs). We are defined by what we are.

And what we are is Text.

The Path to Enlightenment

We did not arrive here by accident. We stood on the shoulders of giants, even if those giants were screaming profanities.

  • The Original: It was raw. It was angry. It was right.
  • The Better One: It discovered padding and line-height. A crucial step forward.
  • The Even Better One: It refined the typography. It was almost there.

But they were all still fighting. We have stopped fighting. We have simply arrived.

The Enlightenment of CSS Grid

Look at the source code. Do you see a <div class="container">? No. You see the body acting as a grid container. We use min(65ch, 100%) to dictate width. This is not just code; it is poetry. It is the browser doing exactly what it was designed to do before we broke it with 14 layers of nested Flexbox.

We Support the Paper Medium

Go ahead. Press Cmd+P (or Ctrl+P if you're on that other OS). Look at the preview.

It's beautiful, isn't it? We strip the colors. We reveal the URLs next to the links because you can't click a piece of paper. We handle page breaks. Why? Because the web is ephemeral, but paper is forever. If you aren't styling for print, you are building a sandcastle.

Hyphenation and Typography

Notice the ragged right edge? Notice how the long words break elegantly? That's hyphens: auto. We didn't need a JavaScript library to do that. The browser has known how to read a dictionary for years. We just had to give it permission.

"Perfection is achieved, not when there is nothing more to add, but when there is nothing left to take away."
— Antoine de Saint-Exupéry

The Dark Mode is not a Toggle

We do not give you a button to switch themes. That would imply we don't trust your system preferences. If you are in the dark, we are in the dark. If you are in the light, we are in the light. We are one with your OS.

Conclusion

You don't need a framework. You don't need a build step. You don't need a node_modules folder heavier than a dying sun.

You need content. You need structure. You need to respect the user.

Now, go forth and <p>.
